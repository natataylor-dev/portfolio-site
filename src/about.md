# About Natalie

<img src="{{ '/images/natalie-portrait.jpg' | url }}" alt="Natalie Taylor" class="about-portrait">

Twenty years of my career sit under titles like Marketing Manager and
Ecommerce & Marketing Manager. None of them say "content designer" or
"copywriter" – but look at what actually filled those roles, and the
throughline is clear. At Calendar Club, The Range, Emile et Rose and
Wild Things Gifts, and through a run of freelance consultancy work in
between, I've built tone-of-voice guidelines from a blank page more
than once, personally signed off every piece of customer-facing copy
against brand standards, mapped user journeys to strip friction out of
confusing sign-up flows, and owned content governance across
ecommerce catalogues running into the thousands of SKUs. That's
content design work. It just never had the label.

The breadth is real too. I've planned and delivered integrated
strategy across email, paid search, PR and social; managed teams and
budgets (a team of 15 at The Range, a six-figure media budget at
Calendar Club); built and launched Shopify sites from scratch for
freelance clients; and, earlier in my career, worked hands-on as a
graphic designer and production assistant before moving client-side
into digital marketing in 2006. I still do the visual work myself in
Canva, InDesign, Illustrator and Photoshop when a project calls for
it, which means the content and design decisions on a project rarely
get made in isolation from each other.

## How I work

Most of the work on this site was produced inside small, resource-lean
teams – often without dedicated content tooling, formal research
budgets, or a content design function to sit inside. That's shaped how
I work: fast, collaborative, comfortable with ambiguity, and focused
on getting good content shipped rather than running a perfect process.
If you're looking for someone who's only ever worked inside a mature
content design system, this isn't quite that. If you want someone who
can build the thinking from scratch, under real constraints, and still
land on something that measurably works – that's what the case studies
here are trying to show.

## What I do well

- **Content strategy & governance** – defining structure, editorial
  standards and ownership for content that has to stay consistent as
  a business, catalogue or team grows.
- **Tone of voice development** – building brand voice guidelines from
  a blank page, then holding the line on them across every channel.
- **UX writing & plain English** – translating complex or jargon-heavy
  information into copy people can actually act on.
- **Information architecture & user journey mapping** – identifying
  where audiences get lost or drop off, and restructuring around what
  they actually need.
- **Editorial standards & style guides** – setting the reference point
  a team can write against, even where none existed before.
- **Stakeholder collaboration** – working directly with founders,
  designers, developers and buying teams to get content decisions made
  and shipped.
