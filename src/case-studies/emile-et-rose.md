<h2 class="case-section-heading">Background</h2>

As Ecommerce & Marketing Manager covering maternity leave at <a href="https://www.emile-et-rose.co.uk/" target="_blank" rel="noopener">Emile et
Rose</a>, a premium D2C babywear brand, I was responsible for strategy,
planning and execution end to end across two annual season launches –
Spring/Summer in March and Autumn/Winter in September – across email,
Google and Meta Ads, as well as affiliate marketing. Every piece of
on-site content, including copy written by a fellow team member, was
signed off by myself against the brand's tone of voice before going
live, alongside my Canva created graphics. Maintaining this level of
continuity, uninterrupted, was the core brief of the cover period.

With continuity the key driving force of my remit, I was still keen to
introduce new promotions for testing in a challenging post-COVID
marketplace. The brand's promotional toolkit had been relatively fixed
for years due to consistent positive performance – seasonal clearance
discounts were firmly established – and introducing anything new
during a cover period meant justifying it with real data, since there
was no room for a change that damaged margin or brand trust whilst the
usual decision-maker was away.

<h2 class="case-section-heading">Tactics</h2>

I identified an opportunity to trial a 3-for-2 promotion, a format
previously not tested, on the brand's best-selling collection: baby
all-in-ones. High stock volumes and a broad range of designs meant
strong potential sales data in order to accurately measure results,
and I ran it over a tight four-day window to create urgency and limit
stock risk. Separately, I led the move to raise the free delivery
threshold from £20 to £50, bringing it in line with competitors at the
time. The change was strategic as well as competitive. A higher
threshold meant free delivery could then be run as an occasional,
standalone promotion, giving the brand a lever to appeal to customers
and drive sales whilst keeping tighter control over cost than a
permanent lower threshold had allowed. I built a standalone
promotional campaign around a single weekend across email, paid and
organic social, and Google Ads.
